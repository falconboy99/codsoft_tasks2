How it works

Features are TF-IDF scores of word unigrams and bigrams plus character 2–5-grams (which catch tricks like "fr33" and phone numbers). Three classifiers were compared on 5,572 labelled messages from the SMS Spam Collection, with an 80/20 stratified split. This page uses the Logistic Regression model.
